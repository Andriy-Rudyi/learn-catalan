How do I turn on audio in the Pronunciation section?

The 🔊 buttons in the pronunciation lessons (and in the dictionary, conjugation and grammar) read Catalan words aloud with a voice that is already on your phone or computer. The site sends nothing to outside services, so your device needs a Catalan voice installed.

  1. Check the volume. Turn the sound on and raise the media volume. On an iPhone, turn off Silent mode (the switch or Action button on the side).
  2. Install a Catalan voice:
    • Android: Settings → System → Languages & input → Text-to-speech output. Choose “Speech Services by Google” as the engine, tap ⚙ → “Install voice data” → Català and download the voice. On Samsung phones switch the engine from Samsung to Google first. Menu names vary between phones; searching the settings for “Text-to-speech” is the quickest way.
    • iPhone and iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Català → download a voice (for example Montserrat or Jordi).
    • Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System voice → “Manage Voices…” → Català.
    • Windows: the easiest way is to open the site in Microsoft Edge, which already has Catalan voices. You can also add one in Settings → Time & language → Speech → “Add voices” → Català, but Chrome does not always see these voices.
  3. Reload the page. If there is still no sound, close the browser and open it again: some browsers only see a new voice after a restart.
  4. Press 🔊 next to an example in the table of sounds or next to a practice word. The word is read a little slower than normal speech, so the sounds are easier to hear.

If a yellow message saying “This device has no Catalan voice” appears, the voice is not installed yet or the browser cannot see it: repeat steps 2 and 3. If there are no 🔊 buttons at all, your browser cannot read text aloud: open the site in Chrome, Safari or Edge.

What is {{ ProjectName }}?

{{ ProjectName }} is a place to learn Catalan: a grammar course, a Catalan dictionary, a verb conjugator and pronunciation lessons with audio examples.

Who is {{ ProjectName }} for?

For anyone who wants to learn Catalan quickly, correctly and easily: from complete beginners to people who already speak and want to put their grammar in order.

Which pronunciation and vocabulary do you teach?

Central (Barcelona) Catalan. Words used only in other regions (Valencia, the Balearic Islands and so on) or no longer in use are marked in the dictionary, with the Central Catalan word shown next to them.

Do I need to sign up?

No, all lessons, the dictionary and the conjugator are open to everyone. An account lets you save words to “My dictionary” and mark the ones you know. To create one, open Sign up and enter a username (2 to 20 characters), your email and your password twice.

How do I reset my password?

On the Log in page, press “Forgot your password?”, enter your email, and we will send you a link to set a new password.

I found a mistake. What should I do?

Write to support@learn-catalan.com with the page (or word) and what is wrong. We fix mistakes as quickly as we can.
